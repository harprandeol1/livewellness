A longevity package flips that model on its head. Instead of reacting to illness, it finds it before it finds you.

Here's exactly what's included, why it matters, and what you can expect from start to finish.

What Is a Longevity Package?

A longevity package is a comprehensive, one-day health assessment that combines advanced diagnostics, imaging, and physician-guided review to uncover hidden health risks and build a personalized plan for your long-term wellbeing.

This is not an annual physical. It goes far beyond what a standard doctor's visit, routine bloodwork, or employer wellness screening can reveal. A longevity package uses the same level of advanced testing that was once available only to elite athletes, top executives, or patients at major academic medical centers — delivered in a single, coordinated day.

The goal isn't just to detect disease. It's to understand your biology deeply enough that you can intervene early, optimize your health trajectory, and live not just longer — but better.

What's Included in the Live Wellness Longevity Package

The Live Wellness Complete Longevity Package ($3,500) includes 30+ advanced assessments completed in a single day, a one-on-one physician-led results review, and a personalized plan for improving and monitoring your health over time.

Here's a breakdown of the core components.

1. Whole Body MRI

A whole-body MRI scans your organs, soft tissues, and structures from head to toe — without radiation — to detect abnormalities that would otherwise go unnoticed. This includes potential tumors, vascular abnormalities, organ irregularities, and structural issues across the body.

Most people in their 40s, 50s, and beyond have never had a whole-body MRI. Conditions like early-stage cancers, aneurysms, and organ disease can develop for years without producing symptoms — and a whole-body MRI is one of the most powerful tools available for catching them before they become emergencies.

2. Advanced Blood Panel

This is not the standard 8–10 marker blood panel your primary care doctor orders annually. The Live Wellness advanced blood panel analyzes dozens of biomarkers across multiple systems, including cardiovascular risk markers, inflammatory markers, hormonal levels, metabolic indicators, vitamin and mineral status, thyroid function, liver and kidney function, and more.

The goal is to see not just whether your numbers are "in range," but whether they're optimal — and what trends in your biomarkers may signal developing risk years before a diagnosis.

3. CT Coronary Angiography

Heart disease is the number one cause of death in the United States — and the majority of people who have a first heart attack had no prior symptoms. CT coronary angiography provides detailed imaging of the coronary arteries to identify blockages, plaque buildup, and vascular disease that blood tests alone cannot detect.

This test is particularly important for patients in their 40s and 50s who may appear healthy by standard metrics but carry silent cardiovascular risk. At Live Wellness, 2 out of 3 patients who received cardiac and brain imaging showed vascular issues not visible on blood results alone.

4. DEXA Body Composition Scan

A DEXA scan goes far beyond a scale or BMI measurement. It analyzes your exact ratio of body fat, lean muscle mass, and bone density — providing data that directly informs your risk for metabolic disease, osteoporosis, frailty, and cardiovascular conditions.

For patients in Roseville, Rocklin, and the greater Sacramento area who are active and health-conscious, DEXA results often reveal important discrepancies between how healthy someone looks and what their body composition actually indicates at a clinical level.

5. VO2 Max Testing

VO2 max — your body's maximum oxygen uptake during exercise — is one of the most powerful predictors of long-term health and longevity. Research consistently shows it is a stronger predictor of all-cause mortality than traditional risk factors including blood pressure, cholesterol, and smoking status.

VO2 max testing measures your cardiorespiratory fitness precisely, establishing a baseline and guiding the specific exercise prescriptions in your personalized plan.

6. Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM)

A CGM device tracks your blood sugar continuously — not just a snapshot in time like a standard fasting glucose test. This reveals how your body responds to specific foods, meal timing, sleep, stress, and exercise.

Many people who test "normal" on a fasting glucose test have significant post-meal blood sugar spikes that signal early insulin resistance — a driver of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, cognitive decline, and accelerated aging. CGM makes the invisible visible.

7. Multi-Cancer Early Detection Test

This advanced blood test screens for signals of multiple cancer types simultaneously — including cancers that currently have no standard screening test. Early detection dramatically improves outcomes across nearly every cancer type, yet most people have no systematic way to screen broadly until symptoms appear.

The multi-cancer early detection test represents a meaningful shift in what preventive cancer medicine can offer.

8. Low-Dose Lung CT

For patients with any history of smoking or significant environmental exposure, a low-dose lung CT is one of the most important screenings available. It detects early-stage lung cancer — the leading cause of cancer death — at a stage when treatment is far more effective.

9. Hereditary Disease Screening

Genetic screening identifies inherited risk factors for a range of serious conditions including hereditary cancers, cardiovascular disease, and metabolic disorders. Understanding your genetic risk profile doesn't mean a diagnosis is inevitable — it means you can take targeted, proactive steps to reduce your risk before disease develops.

10. Neurocognitive Testing

Cognitive decline — including Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia — can begin developing 10–20 years before clinical symptoms appear. Neurocognitive testing establishes a baseline of memory, processing speed, attention, and executive function, enabling early detection of changes and guiding protective strategies.

For patients over 45 in Sacramento and surrounding communities, neurocognitive testing is one of the most impactful — and most overlooked — preventive tools available.

11. At-Home Sleep Testing

Sleep quality is deeply connected to cardiovascular health, metabolic function, cognitive performance, immune health, and longevity. At-home sleep testing evaluates your sleep architecture, detects sleep apnea and disordered breathing, and provides data that shapes your personalized recommendations.

Undiagnosed sleep apnea is surprisingly common — and its downstream health effects are significant. This test catches it without a costly, inconvenient overnight sleep lab visit.

12. Physician-Led Results Review (1:1)

Every piece of data collected during your longevity assessment is reviewed and synthesized by a Live Wellness physician — not handed to you as a stack of numbers to interpret on your own.

Your physician translates your results into a clear, prioritized risk profile and walks you through it one-on-one. You'll understand exactly what your findings mean, which results require immediate attention, and what your most important opportunities for improvement are.

This is the layer that transforms data into insight — and insight into action.

13. Personalized Health Plan

Following your results review, you receive a detailed, individualized health plan covering:

  • Exercise and conditioning — specific recommendations based on your VO2 max, body composition, and cardiovascular findings
  • Nutrition — guidance tailored to your glucose response, metabolic health, and biomarker profile
  • Targeted therapies — recommendations for any IV therapy, peptide therapy, hormone optimization, or other interventions relevant to your results
  • Follow-up testing — a clear timeline for biomarker check-ins and repeat imaging as needed
  • Specialist referrals — coordinated promptly if any findings require further evaluation

This is a plan built around your specific biology, risks, and goals — not a generic wellness handout.

The Four Pillars of Chronic Disease: What We're Looking For

The Live Wellness longevity package is specifically designed to assess the four disease categories responsible for the vast majority of preventable illness and premature death.

  • Heart Disease — the #1 cause of death globally. Our cardiac imaging detects arterial disease before symptoms or standard blood tests catch it.
  • Cancer Risk — 71% of cancer deaths are linked to cancers that could have been detected earlier with better screening. Our multi-cancer detection and targeted imaging changes that calculus.
  • Metabolic Dysfunction — 1 in 3 adults has some degree of metabolic dysfunction. We assess insulin resistance, body composition, and the key metabolic drivers of chronic disease and accelerated aging.
  • Neurodegenerative Disease — affecting 42% of adults over 55. Cognitive decline begins silently, often decades before diagnosis. We detect early signals and create strategies to protect brain function.
